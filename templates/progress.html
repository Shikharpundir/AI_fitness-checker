YOUR BODY-WEIGHT CHECK-INS

Weight & nutrition goals

Log a check-in to see your trend and get general nutrition guidance.

Support gradual weight loss

Build sustainable meals around vegetables or fruit, fibre-rich foods and protein. If appropriate for you, a modest calorie deficit may support gradual loss; avoid crash diets.

Support muscle growth

Pair progressive strength training with regular meals, protein-containing foods and enough energy to recover. A small calorie surplus may help; body weight alone cannot show how much muscle you gain.

Support weight maintenance

Aim for regular balanced meals with protein, produce and fibre-rich foods. Use your longer-term trend, activity and hunger as context rather than reacting to one reading.

This is general education, not a personalized diet or medical advice. For individual needs, consult a registered dietitian or healthcare professional.
